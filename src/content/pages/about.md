I have spent about fifteen years writing software that other people have to
maintain, which is a different job from writing software that works.

Most of what I believe about engineering comes from being on call for my own
decisions. A system you cannot reason about at 3am is not a good system, however
elegant it looked in review. That belief has cost me a lot of clever code.

## What I'm doing now

Building developer tooling — the kind that gets out of the way. Before that,
distributed systems at a company whose name you would recognise and whose
architecture you would not want to inherit. Before *that*, a long detour through
compilers that I do not regret and cannot fully justify.

## How I work

I am slow at the start and fast at the end. I would rather spend a week
understanding a problem and two days solving it than the reverse, and I have
never once regretted that trade.

I write things down. Not because documentation is virtuous, but because writing
is how I find out whether I actually understand something. Most of the articles
on this site started as an attempt to explain something to myself.

## Elsewhere

though not always quickly.
